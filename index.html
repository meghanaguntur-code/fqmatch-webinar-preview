Beyond the webinar · About FQ Match

Solving the physician workforce shortage

FQ Match brings a new supply of qualified physicians into the workforce through alternative state pathways. The platform helps internationally trained physicians and healthcare employers, including FQHCs and safety-net providers, understand each pathway and manage the required steps.

  • Eligibility assessment
  • Credentialing tracking
  • Profile-based matching
  • Compliance guidance
  • Placement coordination
